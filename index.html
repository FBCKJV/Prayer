Printing the Weekly Prayer List

Open 📋 Prayer List → 🖨️ Print, then in the print box choose:

  • Landscape orientation
  • Double-sided, flip on short edge
  • Print, then cut down the middle — each sheet makes two half-page, two-sided prayer sheets

Printers vary — if the front and back don't line up for cutting, switch the flip setting to long edge.
